It is designed for enterprise lenders, community banks, credit unions and non-bank fintechs. Hyperion Mortgage OS (TM) eliminates the "integration tax" ad delivers a zero-friction, audit-ready

Module 36: Offset Mortgage Illustrator & Integration Engine 
Description & Business Case:
Inspired y all-in-one banking models (e.g. Manulife One), this module allows lenders to offer offset mortgage products where borrowers deposits(savings, checking) are linked to their mortage principal to reduce daily interest calculations. The illustrator component provides a real-time, interactive simulation engine for borrowers and loan officers (LOs) at the point of sale, projecting interest savings, term reduction, and tax implications. Crucially the integration component ensures the illustration seamlessly transitions into live loan pricing (PPE) and origination (LOS) and daily balance reconciliation during servicing. 

User Pain Point addressed:
Borrowers don't understand the mathematical value of offset account, leading to a low adoption of high-marking products. Furthermore, traditional systems treat the offset calculator as a separate marketing widget; when the loan is buooked, the illustrated savings dont match actual servicing calculations due to data silos between POS, PPE and Servicing modules 

Potential Impact/Benefits 
High capture rate for premium offset products; increase browser lifetime value (LTV) and stickiiness (borrowers rarely refinance away from an all-in-one offset account); instant differentiation ina commoditized mortgage market. 

KPIs:
Offset Product Adoption Rate; illustration- to-Booking Conversion; Projected vs Actual Interest Savings Variancee; Borrower Deposit Retention Rate

Primary Users:
Borrowers, Loan Officers (LOs), Product Managers, Capital Markets, Servicing Managers
